Two symbols only: × means multiply, • just separates items — never both. Two steps every round: look up which bucket the current value falls in to get the digit, then plug that same digit into the update formula to get the next value. “Show running state” displays the bucket table as a reference (not the answer); after you answer, the worked arithmetic is shown with your actual numbers filled in. Micro-steps breaks all of that into one small question at a time — starting value, then bucket/digit, then each multiply and each add or subtract on its own — so you drill the individual arithmetic instead of the whole round at once.

The digit is how many of the other piles end up above the pointed pile, with those others keeping their left-to-right order. Everything stays face up. Click the piles in the order you would stack them — top of the finished packet first.
